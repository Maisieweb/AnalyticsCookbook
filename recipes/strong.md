# <Creamy Sausage Tortellini Soup>

**Contributed by:** <Makena Strong>
**Serves:** <5>  |  **Time:** <30 Minutes>

## Why this one

My dad and I found this recipe the fall of my senior year in high school. I always loved learning to cook things with him and this dish is a comfort meal for a cold and/or rainy day. It is a great meal to meat prep and always puts a smile on my face thinking about my dad!

## Ingredients

- 1 pound italian sausage 
- 1 smaller yellow onion diced to desired size
- 3 cloves garlic (or a couple spoonfuls of minced garlic)
- 3 tablespoons flour
- 1 teaspoon basil
- 1 teaspoon oregano
- 1 teaspoon mustard powder
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 1 pinch cayenne
- 1 pinch red pepper flakes
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 5 cups chicken broth
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups tortellini (or more if you would like)


## Steps

1. Cook italian sausage and diced onion on medium-high heat until onions are soft and meat is brown.
2. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add flour and cook for 2 minutes.
4. Add basil, oregano, mustard powder, pepper, cayenne, and red pepper flakes, stir.
5. Add chicken broth and scrape the bottom to mix all ingredients.
6. Slowly stir in heavy cream.
7. Bring to boil and then reduce to a simmer.
8. Add tortellini and simmer for 5 minutes (or however long your tortellini needs to cook)
9. Stir in spinach and let wilt for about 30-60 seconds.
10. Salt to taste and enjoy!

## Notes

Great to serve with garlic bread and extra cheese and can be made in any size pot that will fit it all! For leftovers, store in an airtight container and refrigerate for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 3 months. The soup itself is best on day 2 or 3 when stored together as the tortellini absorbs more and more flavor. For long term meal prep keep tortellini seperate, freeze the soup without tortellini or spinach, cook tortellini and add to warmed up soup broth.
