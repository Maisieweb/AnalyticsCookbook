# Gyeran Bap

**Contributed by:** John Leggitt
**Serves:** 2  |  **Time:** 10 minutes

## Why this one

Adding to this Analytics Cookbook has been a pretty rough assignment for me, as I am an absolutely terrible cook. 
In general, when left to my own devices, I end up eating a lot of frozen pizza. Sometimes if I'm feeling bold though, 
I'll make this Korean dish that I found on the New York Times' Cooking app a few years ago, as it's very, very easy 
and pretty tasty as well.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 tbsp butter
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 cup white rice
- 1 packet roasted seaweed

## Steps

1. Cook the rice in a rice cooker.
2. Melt butter in a pan over medium heat until it turns brown for about one minute.
3. Add the eggs and drizzle the soy sauce and sesame oil on top and cook for about two minutes.
4. Add the rice into two bowls.
5. Add an egg to each bowl along with some of the drippings from the pan.
6. Liberally add some crushed-up seaweed on top of the eggs.
7. Mix it all together.

## Notes

This is my dumbed-down take on this recipe by Eric Kim from the NYT: https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1022530-gyeran-bap-egg-rice.
If you're actually interested in cooking this, I'd probably follow those instructions instead 🙂
My one pro-tip though is that this goes great with sriracha sauce for a little added kick.
